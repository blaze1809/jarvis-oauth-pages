# Jarvis Personal AI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

## Purpose

Jarvis Personal AI is a private, self-hosted AI assistant operated for personal use.

## Google Data Access

With the owner's authorization, Jarvis accesses Google Drive through the Google Drive API to publish and synchronize project documents, status reports, research records, and backups.

The application's currently configured OAuth permission allows broad access to Google Drive. Access is used for the owner's authorized workflows.

## Data Storage and Processing

Jarvis processes project information on the owner's private server and stores selected documents in the owner's Google Drive.

The system may also use configured third-party AI providers to process task-related information. Data handling by those providers is subject to their applicable policies.

## Sharing

Jarvis is not offered as a public service. Project data is not intentionally published publicly through this informational website.

## Security

Access to the private Jarvis interface is restricted through Tailscale. Google authentication credentials are intended to be stored privately and are not published on this website.

## Revoking Access

The owner can revoke Jarvis's Google account access through Google Account settings.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application operator through the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
